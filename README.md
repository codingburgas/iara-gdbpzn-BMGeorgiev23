# 🚒 ГДПБЗН — Fire Safety & Civil Protection Information System

> Integrated information system and mobile application for the General Directorate of Fire Safety and Civil Protection (ГДПБЗН), Bulgaria.

---

## 📋 Overview

This project aims to digitize and streamline the core operational activities of ГДПБЗН — including incident management, crew coordination, resource tracking, and real-time field communication.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Incident Management** — Register and track incidents via 112 or other channels
- **Crew & Shift Management** — Automatic availability tracking based on shifts, leave, and sick days
- **Mobile Notifications** — Real-time push alerts to assigned firefighter teams
- **GPS Navigation & Tracking** — Live vehicle tracking on operational center map
- **Task Assignment** — Assign and monitor operational, logistical, and administrative tasks
- **Interactive Map** — Mark fire fronts, wind direction, and unit positions in real time
- **Emergency SOS** — One-tap distress signal with GPS location for injured firefighters
- **Video Streaming** — Live video sharing from the field to the operations center
- **Event Chat** — Incident-bound chat with free text, message templates, and photo sharing
- **Hazardous Materials Info** — Display identified hazardous substances and action plans per incident
